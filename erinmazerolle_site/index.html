Core Philosophy

Our Three Primary Research Aims

Aim 1

Brain Health Literacy

Improving public brain health literacy through community-engaged research, including game-based learning initiatives like the Maintaining Brains Game project.

Explore Game Outreach Project
Aim 2

Clinical Translation of Neuroimaging

Translating advanced brain imaging techniques (fNIRS, fMRI) into accessible, portable, low-cost clinical tools for dementia, concussions, and stroke care.

fNIRS & MRI Translation
Aim 3

Open Science & Reproducibility

Improving the reproducibility and transparency of functional brain imaging research through Open Educational Resources (OER) and open data sharing.

Open Science Champion
